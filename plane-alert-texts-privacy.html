Privacy Policy

Plane Alert Texts · Last updated October 24, 2026

This policy explains what information Plane Alert Texts collects when you sign up for text alerts, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Phone number: the mobile number you give us to receive alerts.
  • Alert preferences: the flights or alert settings you choose.
  • Message records: basic records of messages sent to you and replies you send (such as STOP or HELP), so the service works correctly.

How we use your information

Your information is used only to send you the plane flight alerts you signed up for, to respond to your requests, and to keep the service running.

Sharing your information

We do not sell, rent, or share your phone number or personal information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties.

Your phone number is shared only with the service provider that delivers the text messages, and only so it can send them. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Message frequency and costs

Message frequency varies depending on the flights and alerts you choose. Message and data rates may apply.

Opting out and getting help

You can stop receiving texts at any time by replying STOP. You'll receive one message confirming you've been unsubscribed. Reply HELP for help, or contact us using the email below.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your information only as long as you're signed up for alerts. To have your phone number and alert preferences deleted, opt out by replying STOP or email us and ask for your data to be removed.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of storing or sending data is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
